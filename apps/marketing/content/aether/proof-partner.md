# Help us document the loop in the real world.
We are inviting a small number of individuals and teams with a concrete
relationship question to become Aether proof partners.
A proof partner brings the question, the systems that hold the evidence, and the
authority to review what can be measured and shared. Aether provides a governed
connection plan, evidence-state review, and a documented path from connection
to perspective to outcome.
## Who should participate
Invite someone who:
- owns a customer, commerce, communications, operations, or agent relationship
  question;
- can identify the source systems that contain the relevant evidence;
- can provide or authorize the connection path needed for a bounded pilot;
- can review evidence quality, privacy, consent, and outcome definitions; and
- can decide what must remain private and what may be approved for publication.
## Questions a proof partner can bring
- Which campaign and communication touchpoints explain a customer journey?
- Which sources can be joined without overstating identity certainty?
- Where do human and agent decisions hand off or require approval?
- Which outcome can be measured after a recommendation or workflow?
## What participation includes
1. A short evidence and scope review.
2. A connection plan for the minimum useful source set.
3. A baseline for the relationship question and outcome.
4. A governed pilot or synthetic-to-real proof transition.
5. A review of evidence state, limitations, and measurable result.
6. Optional publication only after explicit approval.
Participation does not require permission to publish customer data. No result is
published without explicit review and approval. Becoming a proof partner does
not guarantee a public case study.
