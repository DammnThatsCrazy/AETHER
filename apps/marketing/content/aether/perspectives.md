# The output is not another event stream.
Aether keeps the relationships that make evidence useful, with provenance,
confidence, freshness, uncertainty, and authority still visible.
## A perspective is governed context
A perspective is a view created from connected evidence and relationships. It
should help a person or authorized system answer:
- What happened?
- Who or what was related?
- Which journey, episode, campaign, communication, or decision connected the
  evidence?
- What supports this relationship?
- What is missing, stale, uncertain, or review-required?
- What can be considered next?
- What happened after the action?
## The four relationship loops
### Human to human
Collaboration, communication, shared journeys, approvals, and the movement of
context between people.
### Human to agent
Delegation, instructions, recommendations, reviews, approvals, and the boundary
between assistance and authority.
### Agent to agent
Orchestration, service use, dependencies, handoffs, tool calls, and economic or
operational relationships.
### Agent to human
Notifications, recommendations, escalations, decisions, and work returned to a
person for review or action.
## Evidence states remain distinct
- **Observed:** a source record was received.
- **Resolved:** available evidence supports the relationship.
- **Inferred:** the system formed a hypothesis that requires appropriate
  confidence and context.
- **Unavailable:** the evidence or dependency needed for the relationship is
  missing or not enabled.
- **Review required:** a person or authorized operator must decide whether the
  relationship or action is acceptable.
An observation is not an identity truth. An inference is not an approval. A
recommendation is not an outcome. Aether keeps those states separate so the
perspective remains explainable.
## From perspective to outcome
The loop closes when a perspective can be reviewed, an action can be approved
or delivered, and the result can be observed afterward. This is why campaigns,
communications, connectors, and SDKs matter: they make the relationship loop
observable from start to finish.
