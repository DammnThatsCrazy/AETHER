# Start with the question you need to answer.
You do not need to choose a package, connector, or SDK before the first
conversation. Tell us what you are trying to understand, which systems hold the
evidence, and what outcome would make the work useful.
## Choose a conversation
### Product and pilot
Connect the systems, relationship questions, and outcomes that matter to your
organization.
### Developer
Choose a Web, iOS, Android, React Native, connector, webhook, import, API, or
agent path and understand the contracts that keep the evidence useful.
### Security and procurement
Review tenant scope, consent, provenance, retention, deployment, availability,
support, auditability, and the exact capabilities enabled for the target
environment.
### Research and partnership
Explore new applications across relationship intelligence, communications,
agent governance, economic perspective, operational decision-making, or
high-assurance systems.
## What to include in the brief
- the relationship question you are trying to answer;
- the systems or sources that hold the relevant evidence;
- the people, teams, or agents involved in the loop;
- the outcome you want to make observable; and
- any consent, security, residency, procurement, or deployment constraints.
