# See the customer journey behind the order.
E-commerce teams already have the evidence required to understand a customer
journey. It is distributed across the storefront, product experience, payment
system, commerce platform, campaigns, communications, support, and fulfillment
systems.
## Connect the journey
Aether brings those sources together without reducing the customer to a single
score. Teams can inspect identity, product activity, cart and order events,
campaign touchpoints, communications, support context, value, and outcome
evidence in one governed relationship perspective.
## Questions the graph can support
- Which touchpoints are connected to the journey?
- What evidence supports the identity or account relationship?
- Where did communication or campaign context change the path?
- Which outcomes can be observed after a recommendation or workflow?
