# Communications are part of the relationship, not just delivery.
Messages, email, campaigns, notifications, approvals, and handoffs contain
context about the relationships inside a journey. Aether connects that context
to identities, profiles, episodes, campaigns, decisions, and outcomes when the
source provides the required evidence.
## What teams can see
- communication and campaign touchpoints;
- delivery and engagement context;
- identity, account, and organization relationships;
- journey and episode continuity;
- approval and handoff context; and
- outcomes observed after a message or workflow.
Aether is not limited to sending communications or measuring one channel in
isolation. The durable value is the relationship perspective created across the
loop.
