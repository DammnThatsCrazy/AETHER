# Privacy and data use
**Publication status: draft boundary for counsel review. This page is not a
signed privacy notice, data-processing agreement, or deployment-specific legal
advice.**
## Product boundary
Aether is designed to preserve tenant scope, consent and processing context,
source provenance, retention decisions, and explicit states for missing or
unavailable evidence.
The system may connect evidence from applications, business systems,
communications, campaigns, social or partner sources, APIs, webhooks, imports,
and controlled agent or service observations when the deployment is authorized
and configured to do so.
## Customer responsibility
Customers remain responsible for the lawful basis, notice, permissions,
configuration, retention policy, source-system authorization, and decisions
appropriate to their use. A connection path does not override consent or create
permission that the customer does not have.
## Prohibited use boundary
The service must not be used to bypass consent, conduct covert surveillance,
create unlawful targeting systems, expose intelligence across tenants, or grant
irreversible autonomous authority without a governed and authorized boundary.
## Required final documents
Before launch, counsel must approve the final privacy notice, data-processing
terms, retention and deletion commitments, subprocessor disclosures, regional
terms, and any deployment-specific agreement.
