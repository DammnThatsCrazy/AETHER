# Data retention.
Retention is a deployment decision governed by purpose, consent, contractual
requirements, operational need, and applicable law. Aether should expose the
retention state of observations, relationship records, derived perspectives,
logs, and backups rather than treating them as one undifferentiated store.
## Deletion and review
The final policy must specify retention classes, deletion triggers, legal holds,
backup treatment, export behavior, and customer responsibilities. Confirm the
actual configuration during procurement.
