# Request an architecture review.
An architecture review maps the relationship question to sources, event
contracts, identity boundaries, governance controls, deployment surfaces, and
operational ownership.
## Good preparation
Bring a system diagram, source owners, consent and policy constraints, expected
freshness, retention requirements, and the first perspective you need to make
useful. We will identify missing evidence and the smallest viable connection
plan.
