# Request a sandbox.
A sandbox is a bounded way to test a relationship question with non-production
or approved sample evidence. It should have a named owner, a limited source
set, explicit retention, and a clear exit or pilot decision.
Describe the connection paths you want to test, the perspective you want to
inspect, and the people who will review the result.
