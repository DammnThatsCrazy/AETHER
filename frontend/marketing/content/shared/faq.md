# Frequently asked questions.
## Is Aether an SDK?
No. SDKs are one way to connect first-party product evidence. Aether combines
SDKs with APIs, webhooks, imports, provider systems, communications, campaigns,
and authorized agent observations.
## What does Aether produce?
Aether preserves governed evidence and the relationships and perspectives that
evidence supports. It distinguishes observed, resolved, inferred, unavailable,
and review-required states.
## Does it replace our systems?
No. Aether is a connective and interpretive layer across the systems that
already carry the loop.
## Can agents participate?
Yes, when the deployment authorizes it. Human-to-agent, agent-to-agent, and
agent-to-human activity should remain attributable, reviewable, and bounded by
policy.
