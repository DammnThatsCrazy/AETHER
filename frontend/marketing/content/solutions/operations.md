# Make operational context easier to act on.
Operational decisions are distributed across systems, teams, workflows, agents,
and external providers. Aether connects the evidence behind the decision and
preserves the relationships that explain why a next step was considered.
## The operational loop
Connect the source events. Preserve tenant, provenance, freshness, and quality.
Resolve the relationships among systems, people, agents, resources, and
outcomes. Create a perspective. Review or approve the action. Observe the
result.
Operational intelligence is not another dashboard. It is a traceable path from
source evidence to decision to outcome.
