# From connected evidence to accountable perspective.
The Aether loop is designed to remain traceable from the system where an
observation originated to the outcome observed afterward.
## 1. Connect
Evidence enters through SDKs, connectors, APIs, imports, signed webhooks, and
controlled agent or service observations. Each path carries its source and
delivery context.
## 2. Normalize and preserve
Incoming observations are classified against canonical contracts. Aether keeps
tenant, source, consent, timestamps, freshness, provenance, quality, and schema
context attached so that missing evidence is not mistaken for zero activity.
## 3. Resolve relationships
When evidence supports it, Aether can connect identities, devices, accounts,
organizations, agents, campaigns, communications, journeys, episodes, and
value events. Confidence and uncertainty remain visible.
## 4. Create a perspective
Connected relationships become a governed view across the human and agent loops.
The perspective explains what can be seen, what is uncertain, what is missing,
and which evidence supports the relationship.
## 5. Review and govern
People and authorized systems can review the perspective. Consequential
recommendations and deliveries can require explicit approval. Authority remains
visible instead of being smuggled into an opaque automation step.
## 6. Deliver and observe
An action can produce a delivery receipt, a state change, a communication, a
workflow transition, or another outcome. Aether connects what happened next
back to the perspective that informed the action.
## 7. Expand the loop
When the first connection proves useful, add the source that answers the next
question. The platform grows from the relationship question outward rather than
requiring every connector or SDK on day one.
## Why this is different
The value is not the number of events collected or integrations enabled. The
value is the continuity from evidence to relationship to perspective to
outcome, with the boundaries preserved at every stage.
