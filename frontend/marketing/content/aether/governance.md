# Intelligence is only useful when its boundaries are visible.
Aether is observation-first and human-governed. Product meaning depends on the
evidence behind an observation, the tenant and consent boundary in which it was
collected, and the approval state of any recommendation or action.
## Capability states
- **Available:** enabled with current deployment evidence.
- **Configured:** supported when credentials, provider setup, or entitlement
  are present.
- **Beta:** usable with a bounded readiness expectation.
- **Pilot:** being validated in a named deployment.
- **Planned:** documented direction, not a current capability.
## Evidence states
Observed, resolved, inferred, recommended, approved, delivered, and outcome
states must remain distinct. Missing or unavailable evidence must not be
converted into zero activity or false certainty.
## Human authority
Aether can connect, explain, recommend, and record an approved action. It does
not silently turn a perspective into authority. Consequential use cases need an
explicit owner, policy, review, and outcome boundary.
