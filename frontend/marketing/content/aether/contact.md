# Tell us what you need to make visible.
The best first conversation is specific. Tell us which relationship you are
trying to understand, where the evidence lives, what outcome matters, and what
constraints the deployment must respect.
## Choose your path
- **Product:** understand the Aether platform and the first commercial package.
- **Pilot:** connect a bounded source set around one relationship question.
- **Developer:** choose an SDK, connector, webhook, import, API, or agent path.
- **Security and procurement:** review architecture, consent, provenance,
  availability, support, and deployment conditions.
- **Proof partnership:** document a governed connection-to-perspective loop.
## Include in the brief
- the question you need to answer;
- the systems that contain the evidence;
- the humans, agents, organizations, or value flows involved;
- the outcome you want to observe; and
- any consent, security, residency, procurement, or timeline constraints.
You do not need to know which plan or connection path you need before reaching
out.
