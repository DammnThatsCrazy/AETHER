# One connected path from source evidence to outcome.
Aether is organized around a canonical path rather than a collection of
isolated dashboards.
## The path
1. **Connect:** receive evidence through an SDK, connector, API, import,
   webhook, or controlled agent/service path.
2. **Classify:** identify the source, tenant, contract, environment, and
   evidence state.
3. **Preserve:** retain consent, timestamps, provenance, freshness, quality,
   and correlation context.
4. **Resolve:** connect identities, entities, journeys, episodes, campaigns,
   communications, agents, and outcomes when supported.
5. **Project:** create perspectives for profiles, journeys, campaigns,
   relationships, value, and operations.
6. **Govern:** require review or approval where authority and consequence make
   it necessary.
7. **Observe:** connect delivery, state changes, actions, and outcomes back to
   the perspective that informed them.
## Separation of concerns
Source evidence is not identity truth. Identity is not attribution. Attribution
is not financial value. A recommendation is not an approval. An approved action
is not an outcome.
These distinctions let Aether grow across new connectors and intelligence
surfaces without erasing the contracts that make the result explainable.
