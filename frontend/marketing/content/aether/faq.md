# Frequently asked questions.
## Is Aether an SDK?
No. SDKs are one first-party application connection path. Aether is the suite
that connects evidence and creates governed relationships and perspectives
across SDKs, connectors, campaigns, communications, social sources, APIs,
webhooks, imports, and agent events.
## Is Aether a campaign platform?
No. Campaigns and communications are relationship evidence. The first
commercial Revenue Intelligence Graph connects them to customer, commerce,
journey, attribution, and outcome context.
## Do I need every connector on day one?
No. Start with the minimum source set needed to answer the first relationship
question and add context as the perspective proves useful.
## Does Aether guarantee identity resolution?
No. Relationships are tenant-scoped and evidence-backed. The system keeps
observed, resolved, inferred, unavailable, and review-required states distinct.
## Can agents act autonomously?
Aether can preserve agent relationships, recommendations, approvals, handoffs,
and outcomes. Consequential authority remains governed and deployment-specific.
## Does a public connector list mean the provider is live?
No. Provider readiness depends on credentials, configuration, contracts,
environment, feature flags, and current deployment evidence.
## How should we start?
Bring one relationship question, the systems that contain the evidence, and the
outcome you want to observe.
