? Questions

Common procurement questions

+Who owns the data?
You own raw operational data, event streams, and records. Tenant intelligence never crosses tenants.
+Can we deploy in our own environment?
Yes. Enterprise isolated, sovereign, on-premise, and air-gapped models are supported.
+What happens at the end of a term?
Export through the data exchange API, then request deletion. Deletion removes raw data and stops ingestion.
+Is Aether generally available?
No. Aether is in pre-production private alpha.
+How is support provided?
Through contact@olympuslabsml.com and a named contact during pilots.
