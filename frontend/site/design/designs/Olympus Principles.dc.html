✓ The principles

In order of precedence

  1. IOperational intelligence should augment human decision-making, not replace it.→ Aether recommends, identifies, and explains. People decide.
  2. IIGoverned intelligence systems must remain explainable, auditable, and accountable.→ Every inference shows its evidence and confidence. Every decision keeps a trail.
  3. IIIHuman agency remains sovereign over autonomous operational systems.→ Critical actions above set thresholds wait for a person’s authorization.
  4. IVIntelligence infrastructure must operate within lawful, governed, and consent-aware boundaries.→ Capture is gated on consent. Tenants never share intelligence.
  5. VOperational trust is foundational infrastructure.→ Trust is designed into the data model, not added as a setting.
◈ Human in the loop

What the product may and may not do

Aether may✓Recommend✓Identify✓Coordinate✓Predict✓Analyze
Aether may not■Execute irreversible, high-impact actions without human approval■Operate outside governed policy boundaries■Remove human oversight from critical decisions
⬡ Data doctrine

Customer data is held in trust

●OwnershipCustomers own raw data, event streams, and records.
●IsolationTenant intelligence stays inside the tenant.
●ConsentCapture follows the purposes a person granted.
●DeletionRemoves raw data and stops ingestion.
