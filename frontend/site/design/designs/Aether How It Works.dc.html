From evidence to outcome, with every step kept distinct

Aether observes what happens across your systems, resolves who and what was involved, maps how they relate, and surfaces a perspective someone can review. Evidence is never shown as inference. Inference is never shown as approval.

→ The pipeline

Five stages, one contract

Every source — an SDK, a connector, a webhook, an import, or an agent — enters the same pipeline and keeps its source, tenant, consent, and order.

  1. 01ObserveSDKs, providers, and connectors produce canonical observation envelopes.
  2. 02IngestEnvelopes arrive at POST /v1/batch, gated by the consent state declared on the batch.
  3. 03Normalize and resolveBronze and silver normalization, then identity, campaign, journey, communication, agent, and value resolution.
  4. 04ProjectA graph outbox writes tenant-scoped projections: entities, edges, and their evidence.
  5. 05SurfaceLenses and 360 views feed the app, developer APIs, and MCP.

● Evidence states

Six states, never blurred

Each claim in Aether carries its state. The interface shows it with a glyph and a color, so a reader never mistakes a guess for a fact.

●ObservedA source recorded it.
⬡ResolvedJoined to an entity, with confidence.
○InferredDerived from patterns. Confidence shown.

✓ Human authority

Aether recommends. People decide.

Critical actions above set thresholds wait for a person. Requested, authorized, executed, verified, and measured are separate states — and where verification is missing, Aether says “executed, verification pending”.

Aether may✓Recommend✓Identify✓Coordinate✓Predict✓Analyze
