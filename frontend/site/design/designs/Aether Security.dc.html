■ Controls

Six controls every record inherits

⬡Tenant isolationRecords, projections, lenses, and cache keys are scoped to one tenant. Cross-tenant reads are not a feature.
✓Consent gatingEach event type maps to a required purpose. Batches declare their consent state; capture stops when it is revoked.
■No secrets in browsersTenant and app ids are identifiers. Server keys stay on the backend path.
◈Human authorityApproval thresholds hold critical actions until a person decides. Decisions record who, when, and why.
⌘AuditabilityDecisions, actions, and approvals keep an inspectable trail, exportable through /v1/audit/exports.
