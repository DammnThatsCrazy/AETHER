# Account Plans

Account Plans capture the Kyber-side success and expansion plan for a tenant.

Fields include current package, target package, ARR estimates, renewal date, strategic objectives, success criteria, risks, opportunities, and next actions. Next actions are owned by Olympus, the tenant, or both and can originate from onboarding, outcome ledger, playbook ROI, integration health, package fit, renewal risk, expansion opportunity, or manual planning.

Account plans require Kyber admin permission and are not exposed through tenant-facing routes.
