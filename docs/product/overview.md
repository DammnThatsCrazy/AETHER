# Product Overview

Aether is a contract-governed intelligence infrastructure platform.

## What Aether Does

Aether captures observations from SDKs, providers, and connectors;
normalizes them into canonical contracts; projects them into a
tenant-scoped intelligence graph; and surfaces the result through
product, operator, developer, and intelligence surfaces.

## Product Surfaces

| Surface | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Aether | Primary product interface for business users |
| Kyber | Operator diagnostics and system health |
| Noesis | Intelligence queries and recommendations |
