# Marketing guidance

Marketing communicates Olympus Labs and Aether. It does not make operational,
security, provider, or performance claims that the runtime cannot substantiate.

- Lead with the correct product: Olympus Labs for corporate material, Aether
  for customer product material.
- Internal operator tooling must never be named on a public or customer-facing
  property and is never linked from public marketing; customer-visible material
  presents Olympus Labs and Aether only.
- On the Aether public surface, attribute ownership as "Aether by Olympus
  Labs" — Aether leads, Olympus Labs attribution is quiet but present. On
  Olympus Labs corporate material, Olympus Labs leads and presents Aether as
  its product.
- Use manifest lockups and reviewed package-owned assets. Do not screenshot,
  redraw, crop, recolor, or place third-party logos from the web.
- Name providers in prose only when context requires it. Registry presence is
  not evidence that a provider is enabled, live, approved, or available to a
  particular customer.
- Do not make provider brands visually dominate an Olympus or Aether surface.
  Pair any provider treatment with its label and applicable attribution.
- Keep capability language specific: sandbox validation, partner live, live,
  degraded, credential required, and unavailable are materially different.
- Preserve sufficient contrast, a text alternative for a meaningful visual, and
  reduced-motion behavior in marketing demonstrations as well as product UI.
