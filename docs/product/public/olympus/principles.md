# Olympus Labs Principles
## Connect before interpreting
The first responsibility of intelligence infrastructure is to preserve the
connections between signals. A disconnected event is not yet a perspective.
## Evidence before certainty
Observed, resolved, inferred, recommended, and approved states are different
states. A useful system keeps them different.
## Relationships over isolated records
People, agents, products, organizations, campaigns, communications, and value
flows matter because of how they relate over time—not only because they exist as
individual records.
## Human authority remains visible
The system can surface context, recommend a path, and record an approved
action. It must not turn an observation into authority without a visible
boundary and an accountable decision.
## Open edges, bounded scope
Connections should be extensible across SDKs, APIs, imports, webhooks, social
systems, commerce systems, and partner providers. Every connection still has a
tenant, consent, provenance, freshness, and readiness boundary.
## Perspective should close the loop
The end state is not more data. It is a perspective that explains what
happened, how relationships changed, what can be considered next, and what
result was actually observed.
