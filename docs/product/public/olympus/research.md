# Olympus Labs Research Direction
Olympus Labs explores how intelligence systems can preserve context across
human, software, agent, and value relationships.
Our research direction includes:
- relationship and identity continuity across systems;
- temporal journeys and episodes rather than isolated events;
- evidence-backed attribution and outcome observation;
- human-to-agent and agent-to-agent governance;
- economic and operational perspectives that preserve native meaning; and
- high-assurance deployment patterns where scope, provenance, and authority are
  explicit.
Research language is not a production claim. A direction becomes a product
capability only when the relevant contracts, deployment conditions, tests, and
readiness evidence support it.
