# Aether Procurement and Assurance
Procurement conversations should establish what is deployed, what evidence is
connected, what controls apply, and which claims are supported by current
artifacts.
## Procurement packet
The standard review covers:
- architecture and data-flow boundaries;
- tenant isolation and access control;
- consent, retention, deletion, and export behavior;
- provenance, freshness, confidence, and auditability;
- subprocessors, hosting, and deployment model;
- incident and support responsibilities;
- service availability and recovery expectations; and
- the exact capabilities and providers enabled for the target deployment.
Formal certifications, contractual SLAs, data residency, and regulated or
classified-workload commitments are not implied by this page. They require
current evidence, legal review, and deployment-specific agreement.
Start with [Security and Trust](/doc/product/security-and-trust) and request a
deployment-specific assurance review through [Contact Olympus Labs](/doc/company/contact).
