# The Aether Manifesto
Aether begins with a simple question: what relationships are creating the
outcome we are looking at?
The answer rarely lives in one system. It is distributed across applications,
commerce, payments, campaigns, communications, social systems, identity
providers, agents, and the people moving through them.
Aether connects those sources and turns the resulting evidence into a governed
relationship layer. It helps teams see the human, the human-to-human, the
human-to-agent, the agent-to-agent, and the agent-to-human loop from beginning
to end—and the transitions in between.
The SDK is one connection path. A campaign connector is another. A webhook,
import, commerce integration, social source, or agent event is another. None of
those means is the product by itself.
The value is the perspective created when the connections are preserved:
- what happened;
- who or what was related;
- which journey or episode connected the events;
- what evidence and uncertainty remain;
- what perspective can be formed; and
- what was approved, delivered, and observed afterward.
Aether does not promise omniscience or autonomous authority. It provides a
governed way to move from connected evidence to explainable perspective.
