# Aether Security and Trust
Aether's trust model starts with boundaries around the connections and ends
with explainable perspectives.
## Core controls
- Tenant-scoped data and access boundaries.
- Consent-aware collection and retention behavior.
- Source provenance and freshness context.
- Visible confidence and quality states.
- Separation between observations, inferences, recommendations, approvals, and outcomes.
- Audit and export paths for governed activity.
- Human approval boundaries for consequential actions.
## What Aether does not claim by default
Aether does not claim universal provider availability, formal certification,
classified-workload readiness, guaranteed financial outcomes, custody, trading,
campaign buying, or irreversible autonomous execution without specific current
evidence and deployment conditions.
## Trust is part of the perspective
The platform should make it possible to ask where a perspective came from, what
evidence supports it, how fresh that evidence is, what is missing, and which
human or system approved the next step. A confident-looking answer without
those boundaries is not the product we are building.
