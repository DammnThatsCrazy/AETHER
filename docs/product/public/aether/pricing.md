# Aether Pricing and Packages
Aether packages access to relationship intelligence infrastructure, not a single
SDK or connector.
## What pricing can reflect
Depending on the deployment, commercial packaging can account for:
- platform access and tenant scope;
- connected event and observation volume;
- source and integration activation;
- intelligence and workflow surfaces;
- governance, audit, and support requirements; and
- deployment complexity and enterprise services.
The current plan catalog is generated from the platform source of truth and is
available in the [Plans & Pricing reference](/artifacts/plans). Exact
availability, limits, and activation requirements should be confirmed for the
target deployment.
## Value framing
Aether does not promise a universal ROI number. The value review should measure
which relationship questions became answerable, which decisions improved, what
evidence was connected, and what outcomes were observed.
