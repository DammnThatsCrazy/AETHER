# Become an Aether Proof Partner
We are inviting a small number of organizations and practitioners to help
document how connection and relationship intelligence works in real operating
environments.
A proof partner brings a concrete relationship question, the systems that hold
the relevant evidence, and the authority to review what can be measured and
published. Aether provides a governed connection plan, evidence-state review,
and a documented path from connection to perspective to outcome.
## Good proof-partner questions
- Which campaign and communication touchpoints explain a customer journey?
- Which sources can be joined without overstating identity certainty?
- Where do human and agent decisions hand off or require approval?
- Which outcome can be measured after a recommendation or workflow?
## What participation requires
The initial conversation should identify the desired outcome, source systems,
connection paths, tenant and consent constraints, evidence baseline, and what
may be published. Participation does not require permission to publish customer
data, and no result is published without explicit review and approval.
## Invite someone to participate
Use the [proof-partner conversation](/doc/company/contact?intent=proof-partner)
or share this page with the person who owns the relationship question. The
first step is a short evidence and scope review—not a commitment to a public
case study.
