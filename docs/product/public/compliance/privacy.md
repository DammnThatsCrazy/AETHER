# Aether Privacy and Data Use
This page describes the product boundary and is not a substitute for a signed
privacy notice, data-processing agreement, or deployment-specific legal advice.
Aether is designed to preserve tenant scope, consent and processing context,
source provenance, retention decisions, and explicit states for missing or
unavailable evidence. Customers remain responsible for establishing the lawful
basis, notice, configuration, and retention policy appropriate to their use.
The platform should not be used to bypass consent, conduct covert surveillance,
create unlawful targeting systems, or expose intelligence across tenants.
The final published privacy notice and DPA must be approved by counsel before
the marketing site presents them as contractual documents.
