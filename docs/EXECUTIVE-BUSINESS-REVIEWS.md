# Executive Business Reviews

Executive Business Reviews summarize account state, package state, implementation progress, usage, outcome ledger performance, playbook ROI, recommendation-family performance, integration health, value created, open gaps, expansion opportunities, and a next-90-day plan.

## Generation

Kyber generates EBRs from tenant-scoped usage and outcome aggregates. EBRs are stored for operator reuse and can be regenerated as fresh outcomes arrive.

## Tenant vs Kyber visibility

Tenants receive a Value Review preview of value and gaps. Kyber sees additional expansion and account-planning fields for Olympus Labs commercial operations.
