# Buyer Personas

Kyber maps buyer personas to pain, desired outcomes, objections, proof needs, collateral, and pricing sensitivity.

## Persona groups
- Revenue: CMO, CRO, VP Growth, RevOps, Head of Customer Success.
- Risk/governance: Head of Risk, Trust & Safety Lead, Fraud Operations Lead, Compliance Lead, Security Lead.
- Technical/platform: CTO, AI Platform Lead.
- Operations/strategy: COO, Chief Strategy Officer.
- Planning public-sector/infrastructure: Program Integrity Lead, Public Sector Technology Lead, Critical Infrastructure Operator.

## Proof needed
Outcome ledger examples, audit export samples, deployment readiness checklists, safe ROI assumptions, and integration effort notes.

## Known gaps
Personas are internal sales aids and do not replace buyer discovery, legal review, security review, or procurement-specific documents.
