# About Olympus Labs
Olympus Labs is the company building governed intelligence infrastructure for
systems that connect people, software, agents, and value flows.
## The company and the product
Olympus Labs is the parent company and infrastructure builder. Aether is the
customer-facing product. Kyber is the internal operator and readiness system
that helps Olympus Labs understand deployment, evidence, revenue, and support
needs.
The hierarchy matters because the company thesis is broader than any one
application, while the product must remain specific enough to create value for
the people using it today.
## What we build toward
Our work starts with connection and relationship infrastructure:
- the systems where evidence originates;
- the identity and relationship context that connects it;
- the perspectives that make the context useful; and
- the governance that keeps those perspectives explainable and bounded.
The first commercial path is Aether's connection and relationship intelligence
for digital businesses. Broader applications—risk, communications, operational
decision intelligence, agent governance, economic intelligence, and
high-assurance deployment—are expansion paths that must earn their claims
through evidence.
