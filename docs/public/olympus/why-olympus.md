# Why Olympus Labs
Organizations do not need another isolated dashboard. They need infrastructure
that can follow evidence across the systems where people, agents, and value
flows interact.
Olympus Labs is focused on that connective layer:
- connect the sources where activity occurs;
- preserve provenance, consent, freshness, and uncertainty;
- resolve relationships only when the evidence supports them;
- create perspectives that people and authorized systems can act on; and
- observe what happened after the decision.
We build the infrastructure behind accountable perspective. Aether is the first
product expression of that work.
## What makes the approach different
Olympus treats relationships as first-class infrastructure. A campaign,
connector, SDK, or model output matters because of the relationships it helps
explain—not because the mechanism exists in isolation.
The result is a system that can preserve human-to-human, human-to-agent,
agent-to-agent, and agent-to-human context without claiming certainty that the
evidence does not support.
