# Aether Product Overview
Aether is a graph-native operational intelligence platform. It connects the
places where people, agents, systems, and value flows create evidence, then
preserves the relationships and perspectives that evidence makes possible.
## Start with connections
Aether can receive evidence through a suite of connection methods:
- Web, iOS, Android, and React Native SDKs;
- commerce, payment, analytics, CRM, and communication connectors;
- campaign and social sources;
- signed webhooks, imports, and event APIs;
- agent and service observations; and
- partner or deployment-specific sources when configured.
The SDK is intentionally one part of the suite. Customers should choose the
minimum connection set needed to answer the first question, then expand as the
relationship model proves useful.
## End with relationships and perspectives
Aether connects evidence into identities, relationships, journeys, episodes,
campaign touchpoints, communications, decisions, actions, and outcomes. Those
relationships create perspectives across:
- people and organizations;
- human-to-human interactions;
- human-to-agent delegation and assistance;
- agent-to-agent coordination and service use; and
- agent-to-human recommendations, notifications, and handoffs.
## The first commercial package
The Revenue Intelligence Graph is the first commercial package because revenue
teams have a clear need to connect customer, commerce, campaign,
communication, journey, attribution, and outcome evidence. It is an entry path
into Aether's broader relationship infrastructure—not the boundary of what the
infrastructure means.
## Product boundary
Aether observes, resolves, explains, recommends, governs, and records outcomes.
It does not silently convert an observation into identity truth, financial truth,
campaign execution, custody, trading, or autonomous authority.
