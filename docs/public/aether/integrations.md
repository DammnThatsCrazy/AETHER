# Connections and Integrations
Aether is connected through a suite of paths. Customers do not need to adopt
one SDK or one connector to get the value of the platform. They choose the
sources that contain the evidence needed for the first relationship question.
## Connection families
### First-party application paths
Web, iOS, Android, and React Native SDKs capture observations from applications,
devices, sessions, product interactions, and consent-aware identity flows.
### Business-system connectors
Commerce, payments, CRM, analytics, communication, campaign, support, and
workflow systems provide context that does not exist inside an application SDK.
### External relationship sources
Social, community, protocol, identity, market, and partner sources can add
relationship evidence when the deployment, credentials, and policy allow it.
### Direct ingestion paths
Signed webhooks, imports, event APIs, and controlled agent/service observations
allow teams to connect systems that do not have a dedicated adapter.
## The connector is not the product
A connector tells Aether where evidence came from and how it arrived. It does
not by itself prove that a person, campaign, agent, or outcome is what a source
claims. Aether preserves the evidence and uses the available context to form
relationships with explicit confidence and provenance.
## Communications and campaigns
Communications and campaigns are relationship evidence. Aether can connect
messages, touchpoints, journeys, identities, and outcomes when the selected
source provides the necessary data. It is not limited to sending campaigns or
measuring one channel in isolation.
Provider availability is deployment-specific. Registry presence, documentation
presence, or a connector name does not mean that the source is enabled or live
for every tenant.
