# Aether Stories and Proof
The strongest Aether story is a documented connection-to-perspective loop.
Every published story should show:
- the relationship question the customer needed to answer;
- the systems and connection paths involved;
- the evidence and boundaries available at the start;
- the perspective or workflow created;
- the approvals, actions, or decisions that followed; and
- the outcome that was actually observed.
There are no invented customer stories in this repository. Seeded demo data is
synthetic and must be labeled as such. A pilot result must remain a pilot
result, and a production result must identify its deployment context.
## Story lanes
- Revenue and commerce relationship intelligence.
- Communications and journey continuity.
- Identity and cross-system resolution.
- Human-agent and agent-agent governance.
- Operational decision and outcome intelligence.
Until an approved customer story exists, use the internal demo and value-proof
process and label the evidence state clearly.
