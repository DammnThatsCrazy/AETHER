# Synthetic Proof: A Connection-to-Perspective Loop
**Evidence state: synthetic demonstration. This is not a customer case study,
production benchmark, or claim of realized customer value.**
## The question
Can a team connect first-party application observations with commerce,
communication, and campaign evidence, then understand the relationship between
the journey and the outcome?
## The connected sources
The demonstration uses seeded application events, synthetic commerce records,
synthetic campaign touchpoints, and synthetic communication events. Each record
is labeled with its source, tenant, event time, and evidence state.
## The perspective
The resulting view shows a journey, its touchpoints, the identities and
relationships supported by the evidence, the uncertainty that remains, and the
recommended next investigation. It does not imply that the synthetic records
are real people, real companies, or real revenue.
## The proof standard
A production story must replace the synthetic inputs with an approved customer,
deployment context, baseline, measured outcome, and permission to publish. The
synthetic loop is a way to understand the product flow before customer evidence
is available.
