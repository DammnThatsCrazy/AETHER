# Aether Terms and Use Boundary
This is a publication-ready boundary outline, not final contractual terms.
Counsel must approve the definitive terms before launch.
The service is intended for governed operational intelligence within authorized
tenant scope. Customers are responsible for their source permissions, notices,
lawful processing, configuration, and decisions. Aether does not grant a right
to conduct covert surveillance, unlawful targeting, political manipulation,
cross-tenant enrichment, or irreversible autonomous action without governed
authority.
The final terms should address service scope, acceptable use, customer data,
security responsibilities, availability, support, fees, intellectual property,
confidentiality, liability, termination, data return and deletion, and the
deployment-specific order form.
