# Campaign Intelligence Overview

Campaign Intelligence is Aether's canonical layer for connecting external ad campaign data to measurement facts. It solves the fundamental attribution problem: every spend record, touchpoint, and attribution credit must resolve to a stable internal campaign identifier that survives platform renames, account migrations, and connector changes.

## Core concepts

**Canonical campaign UUID** — every campaign in Aether has a single UUID (`campaign_id`) that is the authoritative identifier across all measurement facts. This UUID is generated by Aether and is never the provider's own campaign ID.

**External reference** — the provider's identifier (`external_campaign_id`) for a campaign, stored separately alongside the canonical UUID. Provider IDs may change or collide; the canonical UUID never does.

**Campaign alias** — a named lookup key (e.g. `utm_campaign`, `utm_id`) that maps to a canonical UUID. Aliases power deterministic UTM-based resolution for SDK acquisition events.

**Mapping Review** — when an SDK event carries UTM evidence that cannot be deterministically resolved to a canonical campaign, a Mapping Review item is created. Operators review and resolve it, then a new alias is minted for future automatic resolution.

## Day 1 setup for tenants

1. Navigate to **Campaign Intelligence → Campaign Sources** and connect at least one ad platform.
2. Aether immediately syncs your active campaigns and creates canonical UUIDs.
3. Copy the UTM tracking templates from the connector setup screen and apply them to your ad creative.
4. Confirm spend and touchpoint mapping rates reach ≥90% in **Measurement Quality**.

## Architecture summary

```
Ad Platform (Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Microsoft)
    ↓ connector sync
CampaignMeasurementWriter
    ↓ calls
CampaignRegistryService.upsert_external_campaign()
    → canonical campaign_id UUID
    ↓
spend_records.campaign_id (UUID)

SDK landing event → AcquisitionEvidence
    ↓ silver dispatcher
CampaignResolver.resolve_one()
    → priority 1–7 lookup
    → canonical campaign_id UUID or Mapping Review
    ↓
silver_campaign_touchpoint_facts.campaign_id (UUID)
    ↓
journey_steps → attribution_credits → Campaign 360
```

## What Aether does NOT do

- Execute campaigns (buying, targeting, bidding) — that stays on the ad platform.
- Auto-resolve ambiguous evidence by name similarity — a human operator resolves it.
- Overwrite campaign history when a platform renames a campaign.
