# Enterprise Sales Narrative

Aether is sold as graph-native decision, outcome, revenue, risk, governance, and operations intelligence. Kyber is Olympus Labs' internal command system for package readiness, pricing architecture, buyer mapping, and revenue execution.

## Narrative
1. Aether connects tenant-scoped signals into graph context.
2. Aether supports recommendations, human decisions, approved actions, and observed outcomes.
3. Audit exports and deployment readiness help procurement and security review.
4. Kyber helps Olympus Labs see which packages are ready to sell and what proof is missing.

## Safe claims
Emphasize implemented package definitions, auditability, tenant isolation, approval controls, outcome observation, and known gaps.

## Prohibited claims
Do not claim guaranteed financial outcomes or unsupported compliance/certification status.
