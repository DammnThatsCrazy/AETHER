# Government GTM Planning

Government-facing package content is planning-only. Aether remains the tenant-facing product; Kyber remains the internal readiness and GTM command system.

## Approved language
- Government-ready planning.
- Public-sector readiness planning.
- Audit export and deployment readiness gap analysis.
- Human approval, tenant isolation, and governance controls where implemented.

## Prohibited language
- FedRAMP certified, StateRAMP certified, ATO granted, classified workload ready, government cloud certified, production self-hosted, or guaranteed savings.

## Known gaps
No certification or authorization package is implemented. Regulated-cloud and self-hosted materials remain planning/readiness structures until separately delivered.
