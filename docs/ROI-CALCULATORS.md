# ROI Calculators

ROI calculators in Kyber are directional planning definitions, not guarantee engines.

## Calculators
- Revenue Intelligence ROI: retained revenue, expansion revenue, campaign waste reduction, total estimated value.
- Fraud & Risk ROI: avoided loss, review efficiency, false-positive value, total estimated value.
- Agent Governance ROI: failure cost reduction, approval efficiency, governance value, total estimated value.
- Operational Decision ROI: hours saved, delay cost reduction, outcome capture value, total estimated value.
- Program Integrity Planning ROI: prioritization value, avoided waste estimate, operational efficiency value.

## Assumptions
All calculators require buyer-provided baselines and documented assumptions. Estimates must be reviewed before external use.

## Disclaimer
Calculator outputs are estimates for planning and sales discovery. They are not guarantees, warranties, certified savings, or procurement commitments.
