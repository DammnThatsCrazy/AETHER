# Demo Sales Script

A ~5-minute narrative for the Demo App. Synthetic data; no claims of guaranteed
ROI — the value figures shown are illustrative of the demo tenant only.

**Hook.** "Most analytics tools give you dashboards. Aether gives you decisions
and proves the value. Let me show you — with or without our SDK."

**1 · Connect (15s).** "Here's a demo retailer. We're ingesting from the Web SDK
*and* from Shopify and Stripe with no app code — signed webhooks and a connector.
SDK optional." (Click the ingestion buttons.)

**2 · Unify (30s).** "Aether resolves one customer across web, wallet, Shopify,
and HubSpot into a single Profile360 in the graph."

**3 · Recommend (30s).** "The graph surfaces governed recommendations —
retention, expansion, risk — each with confidence and evidence."

**4 · Decide & act (30s).** "An approved decision dispatches to your tools —
Slack, webhook, CRM — with a delivery receipt. Governed, audited, reversible."

**5 · Prove (45s).** "Outcomes flow back into the ledger and update confidence.
Here's the value created and the playbook ROI."

**6 · Operator view (30s).** "And here's what our team sees in Kyber: tenant
health, expansion signals, renewal risk, and intelligence quality — aggregate,
never raw private data."

**Close.** "SDK or no SDK, Aether turns your existing signals into decisions and
measured outcomes. Want to run this on your data?"
