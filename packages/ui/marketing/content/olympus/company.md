# Build the infrastructure behind accountable intelligence.
Olympus Labs is the company building governed intelligence infrastructure for
systems that connect people, software, agents, organizations, and value flows.
## The company and the product
Olympus Labs is the company and infrastructure builder. Aether is the
customer-facing connection and relationship intelligence platform.
The separation matters. The company thesis is broader than one application. The
product must remain specific enough to create value now while keeping deployment
state, claims, and customer commitments grounded in evidence.
## What we build toward
- the systems where evidence originates;
- the identity and relationship context that connects it;
- the perspectives that make the context useful; and
- the governance that keeps those perspectives explainable and bounded.
Our first commercial path is Aether's connection and relationship intelligence
for digital businesses. Communications intelligence, agent governance,
economic observability, operational decision intelligence, and high-assurance
deployment are expansion paths that must earn their claims through evidence.
