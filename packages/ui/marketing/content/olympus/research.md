# Build the context future systems will need.
Olympus Labs explores how intelligence systems can preserve context across
human, software, agent, organizational, and value relationships.
Research is a direction, not a production claim. A direction becomes a product
capability only when the relevant contracts, deployment conditions, tests, and
readiness evidence support it.
## Research directions
### Identity continuity
Preserve tenant-scoped relationships across systems, devices, accounts,
organizations, agents, and journeys without treating a source assertion as
universal truth.
### Temporal journeys and episodes
Understand transitions, sequences, and episodes rather than treating every
event as isolated. A relationship is often defined by what came before and
what happened afterward.
### Evidence-backed attribution
Connect campaigns, communications, touchpoints, journeys, conversions, and
value while keeping source evidence, attribution assumptions, and uncertainty
visible.
### Agent governance
Make human-to-agent, agent-to-agent, and agent-to-human handoffs observable.
Preserve instructions, authority, review, actions, and outcomes as part of the
relationship context.
### Economic and operational perspective
Help systems reason about value flows, operating conditions, and resource
decisions without stripping away the native meaning of the source data.
### High-assurance deployment
Keep scope, provenance, authority, deployment mode, availability, and evidence
state explicit in environments where confidence must be earned.
## A standard for responsible research
We do not move a research phrase directly into marketing as if it were a live
feature. Every transition from direction to capability requires an owner,
contract, implementation, test evidence, deployment state, and approved public
language.
