# The vocabulary and boundaries behind the platform.
The public resources explain what Aether connects, what relationships it can
help preserve, what a perspective means, how evidence states work, and which
deployment conditions still need to be verified.
## Start with the product story
Read how Aether moves from connected evidence to relationships, perspectives,
governed action, and observed outcomes.
## Understand the connection model
Learn when to use an SDK, connector, webhook, import, event API, or controlled
agent/service path—and why no single connection method is the product by
itself.
## Review trust and procurement
Understand tenant scope, consent, provenance, freshness, confidence, audit,
availability, provider readiness, deployment mode, and the evidence required for
an assurance conversation.
## Read stories and proof
Distinguish synthetic demonstrations, pilots, production results, and approved
publication. Every published result must identify its evidence state.
## Use the glossary
The glossary defines connection, relationship, perspective, observation,
journey, episode, human-agent loops, and the Revenue Intelligence Graph in the
language used across the company and product.
