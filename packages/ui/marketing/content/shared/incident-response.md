# Incident response.
Olympus Labs maintains an incident process for detection, triage, containment,
remediation, recovery, and post-incident learning. The process should preserve
tenant boundaries, evidence, decision ownership, and customer communication.
## Customer communication
Notification timing, scope, channels, and obligations depend on the incident,
contract, and applicable law. The status surface communicates service impact;
the approved customer channel communicates confidential details.
