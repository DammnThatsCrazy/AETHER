# Accessibility.
Olympus Labs aims to make its public sites, documentation, and product
experiences usable by people with different abilities and assistive
technologies.
## Report a barrier
Tell us the page, task, assistive technology or browser involved, and the
impact you experienced. Do not include sensitive personal data. The team will
triage the report and provide an accessible path where one is available.
**Primary CTA:** Report an accessibility issue
**Secondary CTA:** Contact Olympus Labs
