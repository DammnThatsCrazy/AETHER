# Acceptable use.
Use Olympus Labs and Aether only for lawful, authorized, and consent-aware
purposes. Do not use the services to evade access controls, collect data
without authorization, make prohibited high-impact decisions without required
human review, or expose another person's private information.
The executed agreement and applicable law control. This publication draft must
be reviewed by counsel and aligned with the actual product controls.
