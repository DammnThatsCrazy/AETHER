# Terms and use boundary
**Publication status: draft boundary for counsel review. This page is not final
contractual terms or a signed agreement.**
## Intended use
Olympus Labs and Aether are intended for governed operational intelligence
within authorized tenant scope. Customers are responsible for source
permissions, notices, lawful processing, configuration, review, and decisions.
## Use boundary
The service does not grant a right to conduct covert surveillance, unlawful
targeting, political manipulation, cross-tenant enrichment, or irreversible
autonomous action without governed authority.
## Final terms should address
- service scope and deployment configuration;
- customer data, source permissions, and lawful processing;
- security responsibilities and incident handling;
- availability, support, maintenance, and recovery expectations;
- fees, plan limits, usage, and implementation services;
- intellectual property and confidentiality;
- acceptable use and restricted activities;
- liability, indemnity, and insurance where applicable;
- termination, data return, deletion, and transition assistance; and
- the deployment-specific order form or enterprise agreement.
The final terms must be approved by counsel before the marketing site presents
them as contractual documents.
