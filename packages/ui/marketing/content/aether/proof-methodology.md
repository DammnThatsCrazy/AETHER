# Proof starts with a question and an evidence boundary.
Responsible proof names the relationship question, source set, baseline,
evaluation period, review method, limitations, and approval owner. It does not
turn a synthetic example, an internal test, or an early deployment into a
customer claim.
## What we publish
We can publish synthetic walkthroughs, approved research briefs, and customer
stories with explicit permission. Every public asset identifies what was
observed, what was resolved or inferred, what remains unavailable, and what
the result does not establish.
