# Connect the evidence behind the outcome.
The Aether Intelligence Graph is the relationship layer that connects the
systems where customer, product, commerce, communication, campaign, identity,
agent, and outcome evidence is created.
## What the graph preserves
- source and tenant context;
- identity and entity relationships;
- journeys and episodes;
- campaigns, communications, and touchpoints;
- conversions, attribution, and value;
- human and agent decisions;
- provenance, freshness, confidence, and uncertainty; and
- actions, receipts, and observed outcomes.
## A graph is more than a collection of events
An event tells us that something happened. A relationship helps explain who or
what was involved, which journey connected it, what evidence supports the link,
and what changed afterward.
Aether keeps those distinctions visible so the graph can support investigation,
decision-making, and governed action without claiming certainty that the source
data does not provide.
## Start with the Revenue Intelligence Graph
Revenue teams can begin with customer, commerce, campaign, communication,
journey, attribution, and outcome evidence. The same graph model can extend to
communications intelligence, operations, risk, agent governance, and economic
observability as the deployment earns those use cases.
