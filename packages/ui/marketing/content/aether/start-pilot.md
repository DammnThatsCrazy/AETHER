# Start with one relationship question.
A useful Aether pilot does not begin by connecting every system. It begins with
a question that matters, the evidence required to answer it, and an outcome
that can be observed afterward.
## A good pilot question
- Which campaign and communication touchpoints explain a customer journey?
- Which sources can be joined without overstating identity certainty?
- Where do human and agent decisions hand off or require approval?
- Which outcome can be measured after a recommendation or workflow?
- Which evidence is missing, stale, or unavailable today?
## What we establish together
### Scope
The tenant, teams, source systems, relationship question, review window, and
deployment boundary.
### Connection plan
The minimum SDK, connector, webhook, import, API, or agent path required to
bring in the relevant evidence.
### Evidence baseline
What is currently observed, what can be resolved, what is inferred, and what is
not available or requires review.
### Perspective and outcome
What the team should be able to see, decide, approve, deliver, and observe
afterward.
### Proof boundary
What remains private, what can be measured, who can approve publication, and
which claims remain internal to the pilot.
## What to send us
Include the relationship question, source systems, connection paths you already
control, outcome you care about, and any security, consent, residency,
procurement, or timeline constraints.
