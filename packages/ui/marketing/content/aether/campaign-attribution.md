# See the journey behind the conversion.
Aether connects campaign and communication evidence to identities, journeys,
episodes, conversions, attribution credits, and value while preserving the
assumptions and limitations behind the perspective.
## Keep the contracts distinct
Source classification is not campaign identity. A journey is not the same as an
attribution touchpoint. A conversion is not automatically causal proof. Aether
keeps these contracts separate so a team can inspect how the perspective was
formed.
## What the connected view can show
- campaign and source touchpoints;
- communication delivery and engagement context;
- identity and account relationships;
- journey and episode continuity;
- conversion and outcome evidence;
- immutable attribution credits;
- financial value when supported; and
- missing, conflicting, or stale evidence.
## Communications are relationship evidence
Email, messages, campaigns, notifications, approvals, and handoffs are not
only outbound activity. They are evidence of relationships and decisions inside
the journey. Aether connects them to the profile, graph, journey, and outcome
perspectives when the source provides the required context.
