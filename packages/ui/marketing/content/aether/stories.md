# Evidence before applause.
The strongest Aether story is a documented connection-to-perspective loop. It
shows the relationship question, the source systems, the evidence boundary, the
perspective created, the action taken, and the outcome actually observed.
## What we publish
### Synthetic demonstrations
Seeded data that explains how the product works. Synthetic demonstrations are
not customer stories, production benchmarks, or claims of realized value.
### Governed pilots
Bounded deployments with a named question, source set, baseline, review window,
and clear agreement about what can be measured and discussed.
### Production results
Measured results tied to a real deployment context, with the evidence state,
baseline, methodology, limitations, and owner approval visible.
## What a story must answer
- Which relationship question mattered?
- Which systems or connection paths contained the evidence?
- What was observed, resolved, inferred, unavailable, or under review?
- What perspective or workflow did the connected evidence create?
- Which person or system approved the next action?
- What outcome was observed afterward?
## No invented proof
We do not turn a synthetic demo into a customer claim, a pilot into a universal
benchmark, or a roadmap direction into a live capability. The public label is
part of the proof.
