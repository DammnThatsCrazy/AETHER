# A profile should explain the relationship, not just display attributes.
Profile 360 is a governed perspective over the identity, journeys,
communications, value flows, relationships, and outcomes supported by the
connected evidence.
## What a profile can bring together
- source identities and identifiers;
- application and product activity;
- commerce, payment, and value events;
- campaigns and communication touchpoints;
- journeys and episodes;
- organization and account relationships;
- human and agent interactions; and
- recommendations, approvals, and observed outcomes.
## Evidence stays visible
A profile should show what is observed, resolved, inferred, unavailable, stale,
or review-required. It should preserve provenance, freshness, consent, and
tenant boundaries instead of presenting a single unqualified score.
## The profile is a perspective surface
Profile 360 does not replace the underlying graph or source systems. It gives a
team a usable view of the relationships that matter for a person, account,
organization, agent, or journey and makes the next decision easier to explain.
