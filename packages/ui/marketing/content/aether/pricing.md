# Package the infrastructure around the relationship question.
Aether pricing reflects the platform scope required to connect evidence, create
perspectives, govern decisions, and observe outcomes. It is not a connector fee
or an SDK tax.
## Choose the smallest useful starting point
### Alpha — Establish the connection
For a first governed connection and a bounded relationship question. Establish
the source, tenant, consent, event, and provenance path before expanding the
scope.
### Beta — Add context
For teams adding business-system, commerce, communication, campaign, or
identity context so the first perspective can explain more of the journey.
### Gamma — Operate the perspective
For teams connecting relationships to workflows, review surfaces, outcome
observation, and repeatable operational use.
### Delta — Expand the relationship graph
For broader source coverage, higher operating volume, additional governance,
and an implementation path that requires structured activation support.
### Epsilon — Scale the operating model
For larger deployments that require more connected evidence, more teams,
stronger support, and expanded intelligence surfaces.
### Omicron — Enterprise
For organizations that need deployment-specific scope, procurement review,
assurance artifacts, support commitments, and a commercial plan matched to the
operating environment.
### Omega — Sovereign
For environments requiring bespoke deployment, isolation, governance,
availability, residency, or control conditions that must be negotiated and
verified individually.
## What the commercial conversation covers
- platform and tenant scope;
- connected evidence volume and retention;
- source and integration activation;
- intelligence and workflow surfaces;
- governance, audit, and support requirements;
- deployment complexity and implementation services; and
- the specific capabilities and providers enabled for the target tenant.
There are no connector fees as a substitute for product value. A connection is
valuable when it helps answer a relationship question, form a perspective, or
make an outcome observable.
## How value is reviewed
The value review measures which questions became answerable, which relationships
became visible, which decisions improved, and what outcomes were observed. Aether
does not promise a universal ROI number before the evidence and baseline exist.
