# Procurement should establish what is deployed.
Aether procurement conversations should establish what is connected, what is
enabled, what controls apply, and which claims are supported by current
artifacts.
## Standard review areas
- architecture and data-flow boundaries;
- tenant isolation and access control;
- consent, retention, deletion, and export behavior;
- provenance, freshness, confidence, and auditability;
- subprocessors, hosting, deployment mode, and residency;
- incident, support, and responsibility boundaries;
- service availability, recovery, and maintenance expectations; and
- the exact capabilities and providers enabled for the target tenant.
## What is deployment-specific
Formal certifications, contractual SLAs, data residency, regulated-workload
readiness, classified-workload commitments, and provider availability are not
implied by the product page. They require current evidence, legal review, and a
deployment-specific agreement.
## What to request
Ask for the current architecture packet, data-flow summary, security controls,
retention and deletion behavior, subprocessor list, availability model,
support path, incident process, and capability readiness for the proposed
deployment.
