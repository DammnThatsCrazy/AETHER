# Document the outcome the relationship perspective enabled.
Outcome reporting should connect an approved baseline to a decision, workflow,
or result. It should show the evidence path and distinguish measured outcomes
from hypotheses or expected value.
## Outcome record
Capture the question, baseline, source set, intervention, review period,
decision owner, result, confidence, limitations, and next action. Do not report
an outcome without the customer's approval and a reproducible evidence path.
