# Customer case studies are permissioned evidence.
A customer story should explain the starting question, connection plan,
governance boundary, workflow, measured outcome, and limitations in the
customer's own context.
We will not publish a name, logo, metric, quote, or outcome without written
approval. Until approved, this page is an invitation to participate—not a
claim that a customer result exists.
