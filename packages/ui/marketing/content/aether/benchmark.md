# Benchmark the perspective, not the connector count.
An Aether benchmark should measure whether the connected evidence helps a team
answer a defined relationship question with more provenance, timeliness,
coverage, and accountable action.
## Measures to agree first
Define the question, source set, baseline, evaluation window, review method,
privacy boundary, and success threshold before measuring. Candidate measures
include evidence coverage, freshness, resolution review rate, attribution
agreement, handoff quality, and time to decision.
No benchmark result is public until the methodology, sample, limitations, and
approval owner are documented.
