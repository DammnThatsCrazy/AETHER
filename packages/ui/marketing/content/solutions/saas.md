# Connect product usage to the account relationship.
SaaS teams need to understand how people, seats, accounts, product behavior,
communications, campaigns, support, renewals, and expansion relate over time.
Aether connects the evidence across those systems and keeps the relationship
states visible. Teams can move from a product observation to an account,
journey, communication, decision, and outcome perspective without pretending
that every signal proves intent or causality.
## Start with one account question
- Which product and communication signals should trigger review?
- How are users, seats, teams, and accounts related?
- Which journey preceded activation, renewal, or expansion?
- What outcome followed the approved intervention?
