# Make public-sector context accountable.
Public-sector systems depend on relationships among people, programs,
organizations, services, cases, communications, and decisions. Aether can help
connect authorized evidence across those systems while preserving the boundary
between observation, inference, recommendation, approval, and outcome.
## Begin with the deployment boundary
Define the mission, authority, data sources, consent or statutory basis,
retention requirements, access model, residency needs, and review process before
connecting evidence.
Aether does not imply FedRAMP, classified-workload readiness, residency,
procurement eligibility, or any government certification without current,
deployment-specific evidence.
