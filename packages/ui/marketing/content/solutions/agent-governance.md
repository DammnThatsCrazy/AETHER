# Make agent relationships observable.
Agents delegate to people, call services, coordinate with other agents, and
return recommendations or decisions for review. Aether preserves that loop so
teams can understand what happened, who or what acted, which authority bounded
the action, and what outcome followed.
## Four agent relationship views
- **Human to agent:** instructions, delegation, review, and approval.
- **Agent to agent:** orchestration, service use, dependency, and handoff.
- **Agent to human:** recommendation, notification, escalation, and decision.
- **Human to human:** the people who own, approve, or receive the work.
Aether can support agent visibility without claiming autonomous authority by
default. Consequential action remains bounded by deployment policy and explicit
human governance.
