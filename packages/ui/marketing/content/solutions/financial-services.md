# Preserve context where the cost of uncertainty is high.
Financial teams work across customer, account, transaction, communication,
identity, risk, operations, and service relationships. Aether provides a way to
connect authorized evidence while keeping provenance, tenant scope, freshness,
uncertainty, and review boundaries visible.
## A bounded starting point
Begin with a named question and a permitted source set. Establish what is
observed, what can be resolved, what is unavailable, which decisions require
human review, and what outcome can be measured.
Aether does not imply custody, trading authority, regulated-workload readiness,
or formal compliance certification. Those conditions require deployment-specific
artifacts and agreement.
